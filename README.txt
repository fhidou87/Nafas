Nafas: installable, offline version

1. Upload everything in this folder to any static HTTPS host
   (for example Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages). Keep the files together.
2. Open the address on your phone once, while online. Nafas caches itself and its fonts.
3. Add it to your home screen:
   - iPhone (Safari): Share > Add to Home Screen.
   - Android (Chrome): menu > Install app.
4. Long-press the home-screen icon for the "Breathe now" shortcut (Android),
   or bookmark <your address>/#now.

Your data lives in each copy separately. To move sessions from the Claude artifact,
use Settings > Backup > Export there, then Import here.

Reminders: allow notifications when asked. Delivery depends on the phone and may not
happen when the app is closed; when Nafas is open at your chosen time, the reminder
also appears on its home screen.

Languages: English, Arabic, French, Portuguese and Spanish are built in.
